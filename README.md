#### Project Overview
Offline lexical URL risk analyzer for QR codes. Decodes QR without fetching link, extracts **24 URL features**, evaluates **XGBoost (400 trees, max_depth=8, learning_rate=0.05) + 5-fold GroupKFold isotonic calibration**, recommends **Allow/Review/Block** via **calculus-optimized policy**.

- **Model:** ROC-AUC **0.9929**, PR-AUC **0.9976**, Brier **0.0297** on **5083 held-out URLs** (1497 domains, GroupShuffleSplit 80/20 by registrable domain, zero leakage), n_train 17057 domains 5985
- **Calculus Optimization:** Minimizes expected cost `J(θ)=C_FP·FPR + C_FN·FNR + C_Review·Review` via sigmoid-smoothed surrogate `FPR̃=mean(sigmoid((p-θ)/τ))` and gradient descent `θ_{k+1}=θ_k - η∇J̃`, verified by `dC/dt≈0, d²C/dt²>0`
- **Real Experiment:** Baseline **0.35/0.70** cost **0.3872** → Optimized **0.60/0.75** cost **0.3676** (**-5.1%**), FPR **7.17%→3.28%** (**-54%**), Review **3.65%→1.36%** (**-63%**), FNR **5.81%→6.37%** (+0.56% trade-off justified by C_FN=5.0)

#### Calculus Module — CIE-3 Core (Viva-Ready)

**Real-world Problem:** Minimize operational cost of Allow/Review/Block decisions.

**Variables:**
- `θ_r = review_threshold ∈ [0.15,0.60]`, `θ_b = block_threshold ∈ [0.45,0.95]`
- Constraint `θ_b - θ_r ≥0.15`, `p_i ∈ [0,1]` calibrated phishing prob., `y_i ∈ {0,1}`, costs `C_FP=1.0, C_FN=5.0, C_Review=0.5`

**Objective:**
- Hard: `J(θ)=C_FP·FPR + C_FN·FNR + C_Review·Review`, where `FPR=mean(1_{p≥θ})`, stepwise → `dJ/dθ=0` or undefined → ordinary GD fails.

**Differentiable Surrogate (Fix):**
- `FPR̃ = mean_benign[ sigmoid((p-θ_r)/τ) ]`
- `FNR̃ = 1 - mean_phish[ sigmoid((p-θ_b)/τ) ]`
- `Review̃ = mean[ sigmoid((p-θ_r)/τ)·(1-sigmoid((p-θ_b)/τ)) ]`
- `sigmoid(z)=1/(1+e^{-z})`, `z=(p-θ)/τ`, `τ=0.05`
- Derivative: `d/dθ sigmoid((p-θ)/τ) = -sigmoid·(1-sigmoid)/τ ≠0` everywhere ✅

**Gradient Descent:**
- `θ_{k+1}=θ_k - η·∇J̃`, `η=0.05`, `∇J̃ = [dJ̃/dθ_r, dJ̃/dθ_b]`
- Regularization `0.05[(θ_r-0.35)²+(θ_b-0.70)²]` ensures `d²J̃/dθ²>0`
- 29 iterations to `0.60/0.75`, history logged for plot.

**Stopping & Verification:**
- `|dJ̃/dθ|<1e-4`, `d²J̃/dθ²>0` → convex minimum.
- Our result: `dC/dθ = -0.16 & 1.55` (KKT constrained optimum at boundary `θ_r=0.60` max), `d²C/dθ²=0.48 & 19.3 >0` → minimum proven. `is_critical_point=False` is valid boundary optimum, not bug.
- Bayes single threshold reference: `t_bayes = C_FP/(C_FP+C_FN)=1/(1+5)=0.1667`

**Numerical Example (Board):**
- Take 1 benign `p=0.2`, 1 phishing `p=0.8`, `θ_r=0.35, τ=0.05`
- `s_r(0.2)=sigmoid((0.2-0.35)/0.05)=sigmoid(-3)=0.047`
- `FPR̃=0.047, FNR̃=0.12, J̃≈0.647`
- `d/dθ_r = -0.047·0.953/0.05 = -0.895` → update `θ_r←0.35 -0.05·(-0.895)=0.394`

#### Results (Real Experiment — 5083 URLs)

| Metric | Baseline 0.35/0.70 | Calculus 0.60/0.75 | Δ |
|--------|-------------------|-------------------|---|
| **Cost J** | 0.3872 | **0.3676** | **-5.1%** |
| **FPR** | 7.17% | **3.28%** | **-54%** |
| **FNR** | 5.81% | 6.37% | +0.56% |
| **Review Rate** | 3.65% | **1.36%** | **-63%** |
| `d²C>0` | — | 0.48 & 19.3 | Minimum proven |

Interpretation: Calculus shifts thresholds higher → fewer false alarms + fewer manual reviews at cost of tiny increase in missed phishing. Overall cost lower → mathematically optimal.
